# Lab 9: Practice Problems

These problems reinforce everything covered in Lab 9: graph representation (adjacency matrix, adjacency list), directed, undirected and weighted graphs, degree, in-degree and out-degree, and edge checking. Work through them in order: **Easy** first, then **Medium**, then **Hard**, and try to solve each one without looking at editorials first. Solving these on the actual judge (not just reading them) is what builds real fluency.

The Easy and Medium problems only need what you learned in this lab: build the graph, then count degrees or look up edges. Each problem links directly to its statement on the judge. Create an account on each site if you do not already have one: most of them (Codeforces, CSES, HackerRank, LeetCode, AtCoder) are free. The UVA problems are included via VJudge, which mirrors the UVA archive and lets you register and submit with a single account.
