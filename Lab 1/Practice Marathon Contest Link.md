## Data Structure Lab: C++ Foundations Marathon 01

This practice marathon is designed to reinforce the C++ fundamentals covered in Lab 1 through practical problem solving.

The problem set focuses on basic input/output, conditions, arithmetic, arrays, strings, character processing, EOF, and related C++ fundamentals discussed in class. The problems are arranged from easier to slightly more challenging to help you build confidence gradually.

Please prioritize **C++** when solving and submitting the problems, as the concepts and implementation techniques are based on the C++ fundamentals discussed in Lab 1. Solve the problems independently and use your own VJudge account for all submissions. Focus on understanding the logic behind your solution rather than simply getting an accepted submission.
