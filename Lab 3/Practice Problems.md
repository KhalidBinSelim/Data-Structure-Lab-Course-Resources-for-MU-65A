# Lab 3: Practice Problems

These problems reinforce everything covered in Lab 3: recursion (base case, recursive case, memoization, fast power, Tower of Hanoi), stacks (push/pop/top, balanced parentheses, expression evaluation), and queues (FIFO order, circular queue, deque, simulation). Work through them in order: **Easy** first, then **Medium**, then **Hard**, and try to solve each one without looking at editorials first. Solving these on the actual judge (not just reading them) is what builds real fluency.

Each problem links directly to its statement on the judge. Create an account on each site if you do not already have one: most of them (Codeforces, CSES, HackerRank, LeetCode) are free. A few UVA problems are included via VJudge, which mirrors the UVA archive and lets you register and submit with a single account.
